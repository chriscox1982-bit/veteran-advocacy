Veterans Deserve Support

Navigating the Department of Veterans' Affairs can be complicated.

Chris Cox Veteran Advocacy provides free assistance to veterans and their families throughout the Albury–Wodonga region.

Our aim is to help veterans understand their entitlements, navigate DVA processes and access appropriate support.

Our Free Advocacy Services

DVA Initial Liability Claims

Assistance understanding and preparing claims relating to injuries and illnesses connected with military service.

Permanent Impairment

Guidance regarding DVA permanent impairment processes and supporting documentation.

Reviews and Appeals

Helping veterans understand DVA decisions, review options and applicable time limits.

Evidence and Documentation

Assistance identifying relevant service, medical and supporting evidence.

Veteran Welfare and Support

Helping veterans identify suitable welfare, rehabilitation and community support services.

Veteran Families

Information and referrals for families navigating veteran support services.

About Chris Cox

Chris Cox is a Royal Australian Navy veteran with 19 years of military service.

With experience in veteran advocacy and an understanding of the challenges veterans face when dealing with DVA, Chris is committed to supporting the local veteran community.

Chris Cox Veteran Advocacy focuses on providing accessible, respectful and completely free assistance.

Our Commitment

Every veteran deserves to be heard, treated with dignity and given the opportunity to understand the support available to them.

All advocacy assistance is provided free of charge.

Veteran Assistance Intake Form

Complete this form to request free assistance. We will review your enquiry and contact you.
