Veterans Deserve Support

Navigating the Department of Veterans' Affairs initial liability claims process can be complicated.

Chris Cox Veteran Advocacy provides free assistance to veterans throughout the Albury–Wodonga region who are preparing new DVA initial liability claims.

Our aim is to help veterans understand the process, identify relevant evidence and prepare their claims.

Our Free Initial Claims Services

Initial Liability Claims

Assistance understanding and preparing new DVA claims for injuries or illnesses connected with military service.

Service Evidence

Helping veterans identify relevant service records and supporting evidence.

Medical Evidence

Guidance on identifying medical documentation that may support an initial liability claim.

Claim Preparation

Assistance organising information and preparing documentation for submission to DVA.

Understanding the Process

Explaining the initial liability claims process and what veterans can generally expect after submission.

Please note: This service focuses exclusively on initial liability claims. Permanent impairment, reviews, appeals and other compensation matters are not offered.

About Chris Cox

Chris Cox is a Royal Australian Navy veteran with 19 years of military service.

With experience in veteran advocacy, Chris understands the challenges veterans may encounter when navigating DVA processes.

Chris Cox Veteran Advocacy is committed to helping local veterans prepare initial liability claims through respectful, accessible and free assistance.

Our Commitment

Every veteran deserves to be heard, treated with dignity and given the opportunity to understand the claims process.

All advocacy assistance is provided completely free of charge.
